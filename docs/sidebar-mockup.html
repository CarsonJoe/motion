Sidebar redesign

Four zones: identity, search, one scrolling tree, and a quiet footer. Only the tree scrolls, so search and your profile never scroll out of thumb reach on a phone.

Tap a page in the sidebar to swap this pane. Use the pill at the bottom-right to switch between unified (threads live in the tree, filtered by chips) and split (threads get their own section).

Mockup only — no data, no persistence.
